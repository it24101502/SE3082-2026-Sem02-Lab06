How are threads generated using #pragma omp parallel for?

#pragma omp parallel for distributes the iterations of the outer loop among the available threads. In this program, the iterations of variable r are divided between the threads, while each thread executes the complete inner loop for its assigned values of r. The output shows that one thread processed certain rows and another thread processed the remaining rows. This allows multiple loop iterations to execute concurrently.


2. How are threads generated using #pragma omp parallel for collapse(2)?

When collapse(2) is used, OpenMP combines the two nested loops into a single iteration space before distributing work among threads. Instead of assigning only the outer loop iterations, OpenMP distributes all combinations of r and k across the available threads. Since there are 5 × 10 = 50 total iterations, the workload is balanced more evenly among threads, leading to improved parallelism and better utilization of processor resources.
